Our Story

Made By Hand,
Worn With Devotion

Home / About Us

Vyshnavam began in 2014 with a single question: could gold-plated jewelry hold the warmth of temple gold without asking a family to open a locker? A decade on, we still start every design at the same place — the carved doorways and lotus motifs of Kerala's temples — and finish it in the same small workshops.

Our Values

What We Won't Compromise On

Every workshop we partner with agrees to the same three standards before a single order is placed — fair, timed payment to artisans; anti-tarnish base metal on every piece; and a hand inspection before anything ships.

Behind every piece

From Sketch To Shipment

A look at how a single Vyshnavam ornament is designed, cast and finished.

01

Design & Reference

Our design team sketches new motifs from temple carvings, archival jewelry catalogues and requests gathered from customers over the past year.

02

Casting

Approved designs are cast in anti-tarnish brass by our partner workshops, using wax moulds carved specifically for Vyshnavam.

03

Hand-Chasing

Each relief detail — the petals, the coin edges, the filigree — is chased and cleaned up by hand under a jeweller's loupe.

04

Gold Plating

Pieces receive a triple layer of 18kt gold micron plating in a controlled electroplating bath for even, lasting colour.

05

Quality Check & Packing

Every finished piece is inspected twice, polished, and packed in the signature Vyshnavam pouch before it leaves the studio.
